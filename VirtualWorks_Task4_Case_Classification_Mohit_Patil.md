## 1. Objective

The objective of this task is to understand the concept of case classification sheets and learn how demographic attributes can be assigned to research participants or cases for organized qualitative data analysis.

## 2. Introduction

Case classification is a method of assigning demographic and descriptive attributes to individual cases or participants in qualitative research software.

These attributes may include:

- Gender
- Age or age group
- Occupation or role
- Location
- Other relevant demographic characteristics

Case classification helps researchers organize participants systematically and compare coded data across different demographic groups.

## 3. Purpose of Case Classification

Case classification sheets are useful for:

- Organizing participant demographic information.
- Categorizing cases based on different attributes.
- Comparing responses between demographic groups.
- Supporting structured qualitative data analysis.
- Improving the interpretation of research findings.

For example, researchers can compare interview responses between male and female participants or between different age groups.
